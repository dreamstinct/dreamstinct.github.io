Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2025

Bank of Todays ("we", "our", or "us") is built by Dreamstinct. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We believe your journal is private — we design accordingly.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Journal entries: The content you write in the app (wins, emotions, lessons, gratitude, photos). This is your private data.
  • Health data (optional): Step count, sleep duration, and mindful minutes — only if you enable HealthKit integration. This data is never shared with third parties and is never used for advertising.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous event data (which features you use) to help us improve the app. No personal content is included.
  • Device information: iOS version and device type, collected automatically for crash reporting and compatibility.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To sync your journal entries across your devices (Premium subscribers only)
  • To restore your subscription and manage billing
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your data. We do not use your journal content for advertising. We do not share your personal information with third parties except as described below.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Secure cloud database for syncing journal entries (Premium only). Data is stored encrypted. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • PostHog — Anonymous product analytics (feature usage, not content). PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Adapty — Subscription and in-app purchase management. Adapty Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign In / Google Sign In — Authentication only. We receive your name and email from your provider.

4. HealthKit

If you enable Health integration, Bank of Todays reads step count, sleep data, and mindful minutes from Apple Health. This data is used only to enrich your daily journal entries. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than displaying it within your own journal. You can disable this at any time in the app's Profile tab.

5. Data Storage and Security

Free users' journal entries are stored only on their device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Premium users' entries are additionally synced to Supabase servers using row-level security — only you can access your data. Photos are stored in a private Supabase storage bucket.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at igor@dreamstinct.org. We will delete your data within 30 days. Local data on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Bank of Todays is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at igor@dreamstinct.org.
