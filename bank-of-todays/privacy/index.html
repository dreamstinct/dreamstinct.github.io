Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Bank of Todays ("we", "our", or "us") is built by Dreamstinct. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. Your personal records are private — we design accordingly.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Personal deposits: The content you create in the app — wins, emotions, lessons, gratitude, and photos. This is your private data and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.
  • Profile picture (optional): If you choose to set a profile picture, it's stored on our servers for any signed-in account, free or Pro, so it displays consistently across your own sessions. This is separate from your deposit photos, which stay local-only unless you're on Pro.
  • Health data (optional): Step count, sleep duration, and mindful minutes from Apple Health — only if you explicitly enable this feature. This data is never shared with third parties and is never used for advertising.
  • Biometric data: If you enable Face ID or Touch ID lock, biometric authentication is handled entirely by iOS. We never access or store your biometric data.
  • Notifications: If you enable daily reminders, we store your notification preference locally. No notification content is sent to our servers.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous event data — for example, how many of a deposit's fields you fill in, the character count of what you write (never the text itself), and which features you use — collected for every account, free and Pro alike, to help us improve the app. No personal content is ever included.
  • Device information: iOS version and device type, collected automatically for crash reporting and compatibility.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To sync your deposits across your devices (Pro subscribers only)
  • To restore your subscription and manage billing — you can view or cancel your subscription anytime from Profile → Plan Details → Manage Subscription, or from your Apple ID Settings
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your data. We do not use your personal deposits for advertising. We do not share your personal information with third parties except as described below.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Secure cloud database for syncing deposits (Pro only). Data is stored encrypted with row-level security. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • PostHog — Anonymous product analytics (feature usage only, no content). PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Adapty — Subscription and in-app purchase management. Adapty Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign In / Google Sign In — Authentication only. We receive your name and email from your provider.

4. HealthKit

If you enable Health integration, Bank of Todays reads step count, sleep duration, and mindful minutes from Apple Health. This data is used only to add context to your daily deposits. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond your personal timeline. You can disable this at any time in the app's Profile tab.

5. Data Storage and Security

Free (Basic) users' deposits are stored only on their device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Pro users' deposits are additionally synced to Supabase servers using row-level security — only you can access your data. Photos are stored in a private, access-controlled storage bucket. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

6. Cloud Backup Retention After Cancellation

If you cancel or your Pro subscription lapses, syncing stops immediately, but your already-synced deposits and photos remain on our servers for 30 days — giving you a window to resubscribe without losing anything. If you don't resubscribe within that window, your synced deposits and deposit photos are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers. This does not affect your account, your profile picture, or anything saved on your own device — those are untouched regardless of subscription status. There is currently no in-app way to export your synced deposits before this deletion; the only way to retrieve them afterward is to still have them on the original device where they were created. This is separate from the account-deletion process described below, which removes everything at your request.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@dreamstinct.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@dreamstinct.com. This is a full, immediate deletion of your account, profile, and all deposits — different from the automatic 30-day cloud backup retention described above, which only ever applies to synced deposit content after a Pro subscription lapses. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request. Local data on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Bank of Todays is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at support@dreamstinct.com.
